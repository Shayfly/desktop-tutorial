V25 closes the historical split between the old V10 product engine evidence and the V11–V24 production/international layers by implementing a new unified behavior baseline.

- V10 parity planner/build/deploy/runtime path.
- deployment-scoped generated-app auth/RBAC/data.
- authenticated WebSocket.
- immutable promoted snapshots.
- source export and safe patch flow.
- Omni Creator Android Studio/AAB CI contract.
- explicit Godot 4 source.
- 19 V10-parity tests.
- 200-case deterministic baseline benchmark.
- recovery/backup governance.
