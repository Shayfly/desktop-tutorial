# Omni Creator V27 — Rights & Company OS

Adds two production-oriented layers on top of V26:

1. **Rights & IP Engine** — copyright/trademark/persona/voice/performer/music risk triage, jurisdiction profiles, evidence records, rights manifest, original-alternative workflow and fail-closed publish gate for unresolved high-risk uses.
2. **One-Person Company OS** — 13 virtual departments with automated recurring work, founder-only decisions, KPIs and a cross-functional release council.

V27 additionally makes Company OS operational: company-signal routing, P0–P3 prioritization, automated-vs-founder action mode, CEO briefs and machine-evaluable release-council gates.

V27 does not claim automated legal advice or worldwide legal completeness.
